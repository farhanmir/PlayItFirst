## Playback

Files are served locally. Nothing loads or plays before the first game audio activation gesture. Music pauses while muted or while the page is hidden. Mute is saved on this device. Recordings share bounded playback channels to prevent repeated events from stacking. There are no oscillator bleeps, generated music, or synthesized fallback effects.

Verification distinguishes decoded non-silent recordings and browser playback from human listening; subjective listening is still required for final loudness approval.
