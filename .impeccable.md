## Design Context

### Users
HackPrinceton judges and friends operating a café together on desktop and phone. The core task is to take, brew, carry and serve a real order, then improve the shared layout.

### Brand Personality
Cozy, playful, handmade. Make the café feel welcoming and alive.

### Aesthetic Direction
Original procedural café with warm daylight, sage joinery, wood and ceramics. Café simulator / Sims build-mode inspiration, with management tools behind a menu. The owner explicitly rejects dashboard framing.

### Design Principles
- Give the playable world most of the screen.
- Make customer intent and the next station action readable.
- Keep payments, inventory and layout outcomes authoritative.
- Protect the player's first order from automation.
- Keep phone controls usable and effects inexpensive.
